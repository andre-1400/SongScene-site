How can we help?

A few quick checks, then a real person if you need one.

Email andrebytkin@gmail.com

Music is not connecting

  1. Open the installed Spotify or Music desktop app and play a song.
  2. In SongScene’s Welcome screen, choose Connect for the player you use.
  3. If access was denied, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation and allow SongScene to control that player. Restart SongScene if needed.

Apple Music catalog matching and optional video previews may need Music permission as well. A missing lyric is not always a connection problem: some recordings have no matching lyric or timing data.

Lyrics are missing or out of sync

Check your internet connection and try another track. A provider timeout can recover on a later lookup. Different edits, live recordings and remasters may have different timing. Include the track title, artist, music app and the point where timing diverges when reporting a problem.

Find the widget or leave full screen

Click SongScene’s menu-bar icon to reopen it. Control-click the icon for actions, Settings and Quit. Press Esc to leave full screen, or ⌃⌘F to toggle it while SongScene is active. ⇧⌘S shows or hides the widget; ⌘, opens Settings. These shortcuts apply within SongScene.

Notch and appearance

Notch attachment requires a display with a physical notch. In Settings → Notch, choose Automatic, Left or Right and adjust compactness, width and hover-only lyrics. Changes apply live. Animation and transparency respect macOS accessibility settings.

Testing builds

The current app version is 1.0 (testing). Welcome deliberately appears at every launch in testing builds. Skip it to return to the player; saved preferences and macOS permissions are retained. This site does not yet offer an App Store download.

Report an issue

Email andrebytkin@gmail.com with:

  • SongScene version/build, macOS version, and Mac model.
  • Spotify or Apple Music, and widget, notch or fullscreen mode.
  • Steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and what happened.
  • An optional screenshot or short recording, with private content removed.

Find the installed app’s version with Finder → select SongScene → Get Info. Please review logs before sharing them; they can contain track names and technical details. Reports are not sent automatically.
