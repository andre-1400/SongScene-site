Privacy Policy

SongScene keeps its preferences and lyric cache on your Mac. Some features send music information to external services. This page explains both.

Who operates SongScene

SongScene is developed by Andre Bytkin. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact andrebytkin@gmail.com. This policy covers the SongScene Mac app and this website.

Information handled on your Mac

With macOS Automation permission, SongScene reads the current track’s title, artist, album, identifier, duration, artwork and playback position from Spotify or Music. It uses this information to display lyrics and playback controls. It does not record microphone audio, inspect other apps’ screen content, or request your Spotify password.

Preferences, connection choices, onboarding completion and dismissed tips are saved in the app’s local preferences. Current playback state, resolved identifiers and some artwork/video results are held in memory. The bundled ISRC-to-Spotify database is read locally; the database lookup itself is not sent to a server.

The local lyric cache contains track metadata, identifiers, lyric text and timing. Spicy lyric entries expire after 30 days: expired entries are not used and are removed when the cache is next accessed or maintained. Files can remain on disk while the app is not running. Apple Music artwork may be written to the app’s temporary directory; networking frameworks may cache downloaded artwork locally.

Local macOS diagnostic logs can contain track names, provider names, errors and technical details. SongScene does not automatically upload those logs. Operating-system diagnostics and any backups you enable are governed by your Mac’s settings.

Requests to music and lyric services

  • Spicy Lyrics: lyric lookup sends a Spotify track ID (including IDs resolved from Apple Music), a shared app-level publishable credential, and an app/version user-agent to api.spicylyrics.org. SongScene does not send your Spotify account credentials or a SongScene user ID.
  • Apple Music: matching Apple Music tracks uses MusicKit catalog searches containing title, artist and, when needed, album information. macOS may request Music access when this matching feature needs it. Optional music-video previews also use Apple catalog queries and fetch preview streams after you enable the feature and authorize access.
  • Artwork and media hosts: artwork URLs supplied by the music player and video preview URLs supplied by Apple are requested from their respective hosts/CDNs.

These services and their network providers receive your IP address and ordinary connection/request information. They can infer the recording being requested. Their retention and processing are controlled by those providers; SongScene cannot promise that they keep no logs. SongScene does not run a backend that receives listening history, and includes no advertising, analytics, or telemetry SDK.

LRCLIB, QQ Music, NetEase and Kugou implementations in the source are disabled in the current build and are not queried. If enabled in a future release, this policy will be updated.

Apple and Spotify also process information through their own apps and services under Apple’s privacy information and Spotify’s Privacy Policy. Spicy’s website policy explicitly excludes its lyrics API; it should not be read as a promise about API retention. Contact us for questions about the integration.

Your choices and local data

You can decline or revoke Automation and Music permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Keep music-video previews disabled if you do not want that optional feature. Quitting SongScene stops its requests. Replaying onboarding does not reset macOS permissions or your saved preferences.

To remove the lyric cache, quit SongScene and remove only LyricsCache.json from its sandbox’s Library/Application Support/SongScene folder. Contact support for help locating it or resetting local preferences. Removing the app alone may leave its sandbox data and system-managed backups behind.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer and email provider receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, API secrets, or unrelated personal information. Ask by email if you want support correspondence deleted; legal obligations and the email provider’s backup retention may affect deletion.

This website

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, tracking pixels, or externally loaded fonts. GitHub receives standard web requests, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following external links or emailing us uses those services’ own privacy practices.

Rights, changes and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of personal information, object to processing, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact andrebytkin@gmail.com about information held by the developer. For information held independently by a music service, contact that provider.
