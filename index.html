SongScene for macOS

Your music,
brought to life.

SongScene is a native macOS music companion that presents your currently playing music with synchronized lyrics, artwork, playback controls, and a responsive visual experience.

About

A focused space for the music playing now.

SongScene is being developed as a native macOS application, with artwork and lyrics at the center of the experience.

Features

  • Synchronized lyrics
  • Word-by-word lyric highlighting when available
  • Current-track artwork
  • Playback controls
  • Animated album-inspired visuals
  • Native macOS experience

Lyrics & attribution

SongScene uses external lyrics providers where applicable. Provider and contributor attribution is displayed in accordance with each provider's requirements.

Privacy

  • No SongScene account required
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No behavioral profiling

SongScene is designed around data minimization and local processing wherever practical.
